3 Download photos, videos and files

Starts automatically when the browser finishes. You can also start early with what has arrived, and run it again later. Close this window any time; nothing is lost.

Speed settings

VK limits the speed of each connection, so videos are the slow part. These apply the next time the download starts or continues.

Good to know

The download is slower than your internet. VK limits each connection to roughly 0.5-1 MB/s, so a one-hour video can take half an hour on its own. VK Archive already fetches several files at once and splits big videos across several connections. If it is still too slow, lower the video quality in step 3: 720p is usually a third of the size of the original and looks fine on a laptop screen. A big account takes a night or two either way; leave it running and check back.

Keep the computer awake. On a Mac this page keeps it from sleeping by itself while downloading, but close the lid and everything stops. On Windows set Settings → System → Power to never sleep while plugged in. Progress is saved continuously, so a sleep only costs time, never data.

Do not put the archive in iCloud Drive. Desktop and Documents are synced by iCloud on most Macs, and a 10-30 GB archive of small files will be uploaded file by file while it is being written, which is slow and can make files briefly unreadable. Pick a plain folder such as one directly in your home folder, or an external drive. Moving the folder later is fine: start the app again and point step 1 at the new place.

Space. Text and pages are about 1-2 GB for a very active account. Photos, voice messages and documents add a few GB. Videos decide the rest: 5-20 GB is typical, more if you sent long clips. The statistics page counts your photos and videos exactly, so you can estimate before the videos start.

Nothing is ever downloaded twice. Stop whenever you like. The next run continues, retries only what failed, and leaves finished files alone.
